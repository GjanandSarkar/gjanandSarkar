02

What is genuinely strong

This is not a rewrite. Several parts of the system are better than what many funded teams ship. Protect them while you fix the rest.

Payment verification

Signature, gateway fetch, captured status, order-id match, amount match and user match. Not a single-step HMAC check.

Stock integrity

Row locks, reservation holds with expiry, idempotency keys and atomic cancel/return RPCs. Built for a festival spike.

Server / browser split

server-only catalogue reads and a service key that never reaches the client. The common Supabase leak was avoided.

Caching instinct

Tagged unstable_cache, five-minute revalidation, explicit column lists, 24-item pages. The right shape.

Trust copy

No invented strikethroughs. No default 4.8 ratings. A support assistant that refuses to invent a refund timeline.

Brand differentiation

Forest, cream and ochre is a real identity — not a purple-gradient template. It needs discipline, not replacement.

03.1

Five things before anything else

Not design improvements. Stop-the-bleeding actions. Roughly three engineer-days in total.

Delete the demo admin path

Remove the endpoint branch and the UI. Add a CI guard. Verify with a production build.

Fix the profile RLS and anon grants

Scope reads to self or admin, revoke anon, and remove anonymous order inserts.

Remove Direct UPI

Default to Razorpay, migrate stored selections, and test that every rendered method can place an order.

Ship or hide subscriptions

No banner may lead to a redirect. Either build the four-step plan builder or remove every link this week.

Stop trusting the browser for identity

Role comes from profiles.role only. Remove email inference and hardcoded addresses.

04

The three modules

Each module works. Each one is also solving a different stage of the same trust problem — and each has a different shape of unfinished.

Customer

39 ROUTES

Strong — product card accessibility, variant sheet, unresolved-cart handling, checkout skeletons, real discount logic.

Missing — no hero promise, no pincode, no slot, no cart in the bottom nav, no search suggestions, dead subscriptions.

Broken — default UPI fails; payment failure has no recovery.

Seller

6 ROUTES

Strong — lifecycle states, KYC, product approval, stock/price edit, payouts, quality reports.

Missing — a “Today” operations brief, guided onboarding, one navigation pattern, payout statements.

Fragile — one 1,699-line dashboard owns ten tabs and six modals.

Admin

23 ROUTES

Strong — breadth of control, audit logs, status transition guards, BI endpoints.

Missing — global search, grouped navigation, money reconciliation, mobile exception queue.

Risky — client-side gate, direct browser PII queries, decorative role permissions.

05

The psychology of premium

Premium is not decoration. It is the absence of doubt at every moment the customer could hesitate. Indian grocery commerce is a certainty market before it is a convenience market.

What users actually read as “expensive”

  • Latency — instant taps and no layout jump say “real company”.
  • Consistency — one radius, one spacing rhythm, one button height.
  • Restraint — one accent colour, few borders, no badge soup.
  • Specificity — “A2 Gir cow milk, 1 L, batch G-2481” beats “pure milk”.
  • Reversibility — a visible return promise at the point of doubt.
  • Human fallback — a named support path with an SLA.

The Indian trust equation

  1. Will it arrive when you said?
  2. Is it actually fresh and authentic?
  3. Is the price honest, all-in?
  4. Can I undo this?
  5. Can I pay my way — UPI first, COD available?
  6. Will a human help, in my language?

Today the product supports four of the six partially, and two not at all: certainty of arrival and human recovery.

Ethical persuasion — and the line

CCPA’s 2023 guidelines prohibit false urgency, basket sneaking, drip pricing, subscription traps and confirm-shaming; from January 2027 e-commerce platforms must publish an annual dark-pattern self-audit. The audit found no active dark patterns — preserve that.

Use

“Only 4 left in today’s batch” when it is true.

A real reservation timer tied to the stock hold.

Free-delivery progress that starts at 20%.

A quality report before asking for the sale.

Avoid

A permanent “Only 2 left!” on every card.

A handling fee revealed at payment.

Auto-adding a “free” item.

A cancellation flow behind three screens.

Fix the failure peak, not the confetti. The last ten seconds of an order — and the first ten of a failure — decide what the customer remembers.Peak-end rule, applied to checkout recovery

Security — the three holes

1. Public demo admin JWT.
2. World-readable profiles + anon grants.
3. Default UPI rejected after the customer commits.

Also: CSP ships unsafe-eval; Redis falls back to per-instance memory if unset; middleware decodes but does not verify the JWT.

DPDP Act 2023 exposure

No itemised consent, no withdrawal or erasure path, identity in localStorage, and a public PII policy. Security-safeguard failures carry penalties up to ₹250 crore. The 2026 e-commerce rules add a dark-pattern self-audit from 1 Jan 2027 and a 48-hour grievance acknowledgement.

08

The six-week sequence

Safety before beauty. Every phase has an exit gate; nothing visual starts until the P0s are signed off by a second person.

24–48 HOURS

Phase 0 — Stop the bleeding

Remove the demo admin path, fix RLS, remove Direct UPI, decide subscriptions, remove email-based role inference, add upstream timeouts. ~3 engineer-days.

WEEK 1

Phase 1 — Trust & checkout

Pincode and serviceability, delivery slots, toast/inline/recovery system, remove PII from storage, consent screen, refund reconciliation. Gate: 20 real test orders across every method, 3 refunds.

WEEKS 2–3

Phase 2 — Premium customer surface

New tokens, customer shell, home hero, product card and PDP, cart and checkout, search, tracking, returns, accessibility, PWA. Gate: 8/10 usability participants order unaided under four minutes.

WEEKS 4–5

Phase 3 — Seller & admin workspaces

Split the monolith, seller “Today” brief, guided onboarding, grouped admin nav, one orders model, mobile exception dashboard, money reconciliation. Gate: a simulated 3× volume day on phones.

WEEK 6

Phase 4 — Harden & launch

Tests, CSP nonce, Redis fail-closed, structured logs, load and oversell tests, backup drill, SEO, analytics contract. Gate: every item in the launch checklist is true.

If you only have two days

Kill the demo path, fix profiles RLS, remove Direct UPI, hide subscriptions, strip PII from localStorage, add a three-second timeout. That removes every existential risk in this audit.
